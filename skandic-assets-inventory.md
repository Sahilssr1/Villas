## Animations & motion

- Hero: looping background video (above) + crossfading hero chapters
  (600ms ease-in-out), auto-advance; headline/tabs rotate between page loads
  ("Wake up on the far edge of the world." / "Wake up in the middle of the
  fjord."; tabs The Cabin / Outlook / The Quiet, or The Cabin / The Sky /
  The Isles).
- "Velkommen / Welcome" loader alternates language each load.
- Sticky nav inverts white-on-photo -> black-on-white on scroll; hamburger
  below ~1024px.
- Staggered scroll-reveal on cards and headings.
- Horizontal "As Found" photo strip (extends past viewport edges).
- Lightbox on "The House" grid.
- Prev/Next carousel in "Before You Pack".
- 12-question FAQ accordion.
- No parallax observed.

## Design system

- Palette: white, pale warm-grey section backgrounds, near-black text,
  forest-green accent (numerals, star ratings), black CTA/footer blocks;
  white text over hero photography.
- Type: geometric sans, huge light headlines (~64-96px, tight tracking),
  section headings with exaggerated wide letter-spacing, small-caps eyebrow
  labels ("01—INTRODUCTION"), small grey body copy.
- Buttons: pills — white on hero photo, black on light sections, white on
  black — usually with a ↗ arrow.
- Nav: left wordmark "Skandic" + "LOFOTEN, NORWAY", centre links, right
  pill "Enquire ↗".
- Cards: large rounded corners, hairline dividers, generous whitespace,
  green star ratings.
- Footer (black): giant blurred "Skandic" wordmark, link columns,
  © 2026 Skandic, Lofoten, Norway.
